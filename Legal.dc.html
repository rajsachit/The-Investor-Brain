01Privacy

We collect only what we need to run your course: your name, phone number, email, the course you choose, your experience level and any notes you share with us. We never sell your data.

We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

02How we use it

To book your free class, match you with a mentor, schedule classes, send class notes and answer your questions. Mentors see only what they need to teach you.

03Payments & GST

All course fees are in Indian rupees (₹). GST is charged as applicable and shown on your invoice.

Payments are handled by our payment gateway. We never store your card, UPI or bank details.

04Refunds

The first class is free and carries no obligation. If a paid course is not right for you, write to us within 7 days of your first paid class and we will refund the unused part of your fee.

05Not investment advice

Our courses are for education only. Nothing in a class, worksheet or message is a recommendation to buy or sell any security, and we do not give stock tips or promise returns.

Investments in the securities market are subject to market risks. Read all related documents carefully before investing, and every investment decision you make is your own.

06Your choices

Ask us any time to see, correct or delete your details, or to withdraw your consent — email the front desk. Questions or complaints about your data can be sent to our grievance officer at the same address.

07Terms

Classes can be moved up to 24 hours before without charge. Course material and recordings are for your personal use only and may not be shared or resold.

08Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any dispute is subject to the courts of [your city].
